dessert

ingredients

  • Mashed potatoes: Cold, leftover mashed potatoes work best.
  • Egg
  • Flour
  • Cheese
  • Breadcrumbs
  • Green onions/chives
  • oil

    • steps

    1. Combine cooled mashed potatoes with desired mix-ins in a bowl. The mixture should be firm enough to shape.
    2. Form the mixture into uniform balls
    3. Chill the formed balls for at least 15-30 minutes to help them hold their shape.
    4. Coat each potato ball by rolling in flour, dipping in egg, and then coating in breadcrumbs.
    5. Heat oil to about 350°F (175°C). Carefully fry the coated balls in batches for 2-3 minutes, or until golden brown and crispy
    6. Drain on paper towels and season with salt. Serve warm
