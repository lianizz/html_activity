breakfast

ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati rice.
  • 3 numbers eggs.
  • ½ cup carrot finely chopped
  • ½ cup capsicum.
  • ¾ cup cabbage.
  • ¼ cup beans finely chopped.
  • ¼ cup spring onions - green part finely chopped.
  • 1 tablespoon spring onions - white part finely chopped.

    • steps

    1. Heat your oil in a heavy bottomed frying pan.
    2. Add onions, garlic, ginger, five spice, the vegetables and fry over a high heat for 4 minutes.
    3. Pour in your rice, oyster sauce and soy sauce and fry for a further 3 minutes.
    4. Whisk your eggs, make a couple of wells in the mixture and add the eggs.
