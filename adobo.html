ingredients

  • Chicken: Typically cut into serving pieces
  • Soy sauce: A key component for the savory, dark base
  • Vinegar: Provides the dish's signature tang
  • Garlic: Used generously and usually crushed or chopped
  • Peppercorns: Whole black peppercorns are used for their potent flavor

steps

  1. Heat atsuete oil in a pot. Sear your meat until brown.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, and onion. Cook until fragrant.
  3. Add soy sauce, vinegar, and water. Simmer until the sauce reduces.
  4. Season with cracked pepper, bay leaves, patis, and brown sugar. Serve immediately.
