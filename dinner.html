dinner

ingredients

  • Pork: Pork belly or pork ribs, cut into bite-sized pieces.
  • Souring Agent: Instant tamarind soup mix (like Knorr or Mama Sita) or fresh tamarind fruit.
  • Aromatics: Onion and tomatoes
  • Vegetables.
  • Seasoning
  • Liquid

    • steps

    1. Pour water into a cooking pot. Let boil.
    2. Add onion and tomato. Boil for 5 to 8 minutes.
    3. Put the pork in the cooking pot. ...
    4. Add Knorr Sinigang sa Sampaloc mix, fish sauce, and ground black pepper; stir..
    5. Add the vegetables one by one
    6. Cover the cooking pot and turn off the heat. Let it stay covered for 5 minutes.
    7. Transfer to as serving bowl. Serve with a small bowl with patis and spicy chili.
    8. Share and enjoy!
